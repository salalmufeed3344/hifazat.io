# Presentation Plan — Presenting Hifazat at a High Level in Pakistan

## The story (60 seconds, Urdu first)

"PECA Section 21 already makes AI-editing someone's photo into something illegal a crime — up to 7 years. FIA got 1,180 deepfake complaints in 2023. But a girl in Mardan can't prove which photo is real, can't protect her voice note from being cloned, and can't stop her photo being edited in Gemini. Hifazat does all of that **before the photo leaves her phone** — free, in Urdu, on a low-end phone. And if a fake still circulates, it gives her a ready-to-file evidence pack for NCCIA 1799."

## The 5-minute demo flow

1. **Nishan (60 s):** Register a photo → show a fake → match → print the Urdu evidence pack.
2. **Awaaz (60 s):** Play a cloned voice (sounds real) → play clone from protected voice (garbled). Audience hears it.
3. **Tainted Video (60 s):** Original video (normal) → face-swap of it (stripes expose the fake).
4. **MIRAGE (90 s):** "Edit this photo" in ChatGPT/Gemini on unprotected (succeeds) → same prompt on protected (**refuses**, live).
5. **Numbers + honest limits (60 s):** Test matrix on screen. "This raises the bar and creates evidence. It does not guarantee safety. Here's what survives WhatsApp and what doesn't."

Fallback: every live demo has a dated screen recording. If booth internet dies, say so and play the recording — labelled, never faked as live.

## Poster structure

- Top: problem in one line + PECA/FIA numbers with sources.
- Middle: 4 modules, one icon + one line each, before/after visuals.
- Bottom: test matrix table (measured, dated) + "built by an 18-year-old self-taught builder in Pakistan" + QR to live site.

## What judges will ask — prepare answers

- "Isn't this just PhotoGuard?" → PhotoGuard is 2023, SD 1.5 only, fails vs Gemini/ChatGPT (Mar 2026 study). MIRAGE attacks the moderator, not the generator. Show the baseline test.
- "Didn't NUST already do this?" → NUST RAPID is a research paper + code, 0.10 s/image, no usable tool. We build the product layer: Urdu, WhatsApp-tested, evidence pack, four surfaces (photo/voice/video/proof). Cite RAPID, don't hide it.
- "Can it be stripped?" → Yes, partly. Show the robustness battery numbers. That's why Nishan (proof) exists — defence in depth, like an immune system.
- "Why Pakistan?" → PECA §21, honour-based risk, WhatsApp-first sharing, Urdu-first users, low-end phones. Designed for those constraints, not adapted to them.
